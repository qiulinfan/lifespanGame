# Demonstration guide

## Recommended route

This route produces the **Responsive Guide** ending while showing that the
best response changes with the situation.

1. Opening belief: choose any answer. It is not scored.
2. Age 4, moment 1: choose **1** — ask what could make the base stronger.
3. Age 4, moment 2: choose **1** — wait and let Alex test the idea.
4. Age 9, moment 1: choose **1** — identify what is already working.
5. Age 9, moment 2: choose **4** — solve one section first.
6. Age 15, moment 1: choose **1** — ask what kind of support would help.
7. Age 15, moment 2: choose **1** — agree on a boundary and let Alex lead.
8. At the ending, open **View Concepts**.

## Suggested narration

- Introduce the myth: more help is not automatically better help.
- Explain that the four bars separate immediate task completion from longer
  developmental outcomes.
- Point out how taking over can raise Progress while lowering Independence.
- In the age-4 chapter, name the ZPD and scaffolding.
- In the age-9 chapter, connect feedback to industry versus inferiority.
- In the age-15 chapter, connect responsive structure to autonomy and identity
  exploration.
- End with the main claim: observe first, help with the next step, and reduce
  support as competence grows.

## Optional comparison shot

For a quick contrast, replay age 4 and select **Build the bridge**. The product
finishes faster, but the result text and status bars reveal the lost practice
opportunity. This is the clearest single shot for demonstrating the game's
central mechanic.
